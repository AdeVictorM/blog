This is the first post on the blog. Nothing fancy — just a place to write
things down that would otherwise get lost in a notes app somewhere.

## Why a blog

A few reasons:

- Writing something down properly forces you to actually understand it
- It's a public record you can point people to instead of re-explaining things
- It's a good excuse to keep building on this little site

## How it's built

Every post here is a plain Markdown file with a small front-matter header
at the top for the title, date, excerpt, and tags. No CMS, no database —
just files.

```
---
title: My Post
date: 2026-01-01
excerpt: A short summary shown on the home page.
tags: [example]
---

Post content goes here, in **Markdown**.
```

> Simple tools, used consistently, beat complicated tools you have to fight
> with every time.
